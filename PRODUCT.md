# PRODUCT.md — Mentorship MetaHub (Meta-Analysis Academy)

> Drafted from the live product (mentorship.metaanalysisacademy.com) and the current design prototype. Review and refine with `impeccable teach`.

## Product purpose

Web platform where Meta-Analysis Academy mentees run their entire mentorship: book weekly sessions with their mentor, follow a fixed 8-stage research pipeline (MetaTracker, 37 tasks from research question to publication), attend office hours, and watch course content. Mentors use the same app to manage availability, mentees, and session outcomes.

## Register

product

## Users

- **Mentees**: Brazilian and international medical students, residents, and MDs (often ESL) doing their first meta-analysis. Motivated but time-poor; use the app between clinical duties, mostly desktop, sometimes mobile.
- **Mentors**: physician-researchers (e.g. Vânio Antunes) handling 5–12 mentees each; need fast scheduling, outcome recording, and per-mentee progress at a glance.

## Brand & tone

- Academic-warm, not corporate SaaS. Cream/parchment surfaces, deep navy primary (hsl(234 85% 14%)), Ruda for display, JetBrains Mono for times/data.
- Copy is direct, encouraging, concrete ("Book your weekly mentorship session"), sentence case, separator idiom is "·". No em dashes.
- Trustworthy and calm: mentees are anxious first-time researchers; the interface should feel like a well-organized supervisor, not a growth-hacked app.

## Strategic principles

1. The weekly session is the heartbeat: next meeting and booking are always one glance away.
2. Progress is linear and visible: the 8-stage journey from question to publication anchors every research view.
3. Timezone honesty: every time is shown in the viewer's timezone, conversion is explicit.
4. Mentor time is scarce: mentor views optimize for batch actions (availability, outcomes, roster).

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (hero metrics, gradient cards, purple-on-dark).
- LMS clutter (Moodle-style link piles).
- Calendly-style sterile booking with no relationship context.
